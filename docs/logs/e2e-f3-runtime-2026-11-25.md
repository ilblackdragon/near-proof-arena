## Findings

* **F3-1 (resume).** The resume AIR carries `stRoot`, the accumulators and `vmAcc`; for any view
  whose state commitment sees the step, its output digest cannot open: the relative obligation is
  unprovable for that AIR. Needs the lane B split, in-circuit accumulator appends (SHA-384) and
  the `estepO` composition. Named premise `SplitC.RtOthers`.
* **F3-2 (clear fields).** `ViewE.clearE` sets all counters to 0, the resume leaf ties them to
  `RX`'s totals, lane I's driver fills them with the run's counters: an honest resume leaf
  cannot meet `clearE`. Lane E / owner decision. No change of `S`, `claimDigest`, checker.
* **S2 in lane A's view.** `WasmView.fbBnd` still has `codeRoot = bdigest []`; `RtView.preW` (the
  S2 `.pre` record) is lane A's instruction from M11i, not yet in lane A's file.
* **STATE.** Lane F has no VM-free engine rows; `EngHalfRC` for STATE has no AIR. Stated only.
