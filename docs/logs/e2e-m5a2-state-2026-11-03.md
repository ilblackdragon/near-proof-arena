## Done

* **Batched FINAL in the AIR.** Each trie node is hashed once: a pre-state node by its first
  reader (published on a new `VER` bus under its digest for every other reader — INTAKE is
  batched too), a node a later change rewrites is published under a version id (the producing
  row's id) and never hashed, a final node is hashed by its row. Child slots, `DOWN`/`UP` and the
  root chain `TCH` carry refs (digest | version id). No timestamps: records are immutable and ids
  unique, so `VER` is a persistent store.
* **T1 and T2 re-proved** on the main stack, roots equal to the Lean runs' `stateRoot` and to
  the Lean spec oracle (`m5a-tool apply`): T1 1,258,042 B, 268.2-bit, prove 260 s, verify 179 ms,
  1.5 GB; T2 1,296,042 B (was 1,533,468), 268.2-bit, prove 478 s (was 4,392 s throttled), verify
  190 ms, 2.7 GB (was 16.4 GB).
* **Cost:** trie-row SHA-256 cells −67 % (T1) / −86 % (T2); total used cells 8.15 M → 4.83 M
  and 34.25 M → 14.97 M; committed 14.5 M → 7.9 M and 47.3 M → 16.2 M. The SHA-512 working-set
  chip now dominates.
* **Mutants:** T2 17/17, T1 9/9, synthetic 7/7 rejected on the final AIR (the 12 of M5a plus
  `verslot`, `vernid`, `vmcount`, `finroot`, `nidforge`; `bvalue`, `bvmem` on the synthetic case).
* **Completeness:** branch values (`BR.oUV` write, `BR.oDV` delete keeping ≥ 2 children,
  `BR.oDZ` absent delete), tested on a synthetic NEAR-shaped case (`synth.py bval`) whose root the
  Lean spec confirms; it proves (1,237,614 B, 130 s).
* **Soundness fix found while building:** a published (unhashed) node's memory bytes are no longer
  range-checked by the SHA table; they now go through `RNG` (as do a written value's length bytes).
* **Lean** (std axioms, no `sorry`): `Store` (store expansion is functional; reveal / collapse keep
  refinement; `batched_changes`: through every upsert and delete the post root is `rootHash` of
  `applyChanges` on the witness, premises `Sha256CR` + `KeepsFW`), `FieldVer`, `StepsBV`; Lean
  sub-lane: **`NodeSerInj` proved** (`SerInj.nodeSerInj`; `Shallow` requires ref slots),
  **`del_refinedBy`** for the general spec `del`, `rows_refinedBy` (rows' trie refines the full
  trie from the root hash, `Sha256CR` only), `sn_hexPrefix`, `br_sel`.
