## Not done, and why

Items 2 (`SeStep`: VM-free engine rows), 3 (`encRestI`) and 4 (`RsStep`'s `estepO` composition)
share one blocker: the leaf must open the configuration it steps from, and on the chain
`ctxRoot` (`ctxD (.intake I)`) and `stRoot.rest` are `0^42` placeholders; lane B's `STC` passes `rest`
opaque and its `tag` does not distinguish `yields` from other engine positions. Rows computing the
steps now would compute a step of a configuration the record does not commit to, so they cannot
discharge `SeStep` (`FinPre`'s `c.pos = .yields _ _ 0`, `Meaning .state` from that `c`) or
`RsStep` (the executed receipt is `c`'s queue head). `encRestI` itself is not adopted: the
per-cut values need T1's state component at each cut (lane E's `restOfB`, not in the chain's
inputs), and adopting them moves `stRoot` at c1 … c9, so lane B's five state halves, lane F's five
leaves and lane D's running M10 tree would all be re-proved, for no soundness gain while `rest` is a
witnessed meta. Proposed order: E puts the per-cut `encRestI` values in `chain.py`; B re-proves; F
builds the rows (`d_queue` pops, `d_move`, `ACC` appends, counters, `SREQ`/`HB`) and proves
`SeStep` / `RsStep` from them with `encRestI_bind` (README §F-6).
