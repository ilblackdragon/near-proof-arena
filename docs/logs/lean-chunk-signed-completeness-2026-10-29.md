## What was done

1. Baseline `--tier signed` on the census (3,138), every committed fixture dir (593), the 338 cases
   in the evidence `cases.tar.gz` archives, mainnet shard 13 (1,223), the six raw split runs of
   `resharding-fixtures` (1,173 PV86 cases), and the corpus (235,716).
2. Findings beyond the workplan's list:
   * 26 nearcore-valid `storage-limit-fixtures` chunks were **rejected** by the signed tier
     (`MissingTrieValue`). The legacy engine assumes the storage-proof limits never trigger. It checks
     that assumption (`w.size`) only *after* the main transition, and nearcore's postponed receipts
     leave values out of the witness. Reproduced on a regenerated storage-limit chain (23 of 1,200).
   * Of the 10 committed `fuel exhausted` fixtures, 4 exceed `G_α`. They move to `e.g_alpha`, not to
     accept.
   * No case in any population reaches the legacy "outgoing buffer to a shard outside the layout"
     exclusion.
3. Design: `checkD3` cannot call `ReshardCore` from `D3/FunctionCall.lean`, because `ReshardCore`
   imports it. The old body became `checkD3Core`, and `checkD3` moved to the new `D3/Signed.lean`.
   `ChallengeChunkV3.lean` needs one import line for it. The hand-over fires only on core errors, so
   every core accept is kept, as proved.
4. The proof updates this needed: `TierMono`, the `Config` adapter theorems, and four `zk-formal`
   join files. In each, a theorem that relied on `RelD3 ≡ checkD2Core …` is restated on
   `checkD3Core`, or reaches `RelD3` through `checkD3_of_core_ok`.
5. (c) stopped. It is a definition of the signed challenge. The proposal is in the evidence README.
6. (d) `Wasm/DomainD3.lean` deleted. `noResharding` moved to `ReshardingD3.lean`, and the coverage tool
   was updated (the JSON is not regenerated).
