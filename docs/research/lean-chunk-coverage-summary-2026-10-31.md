**Which relation the statuses describe.** Every status now describes the canonical Gate-B relation,
the successor tier **`allFeatures`** (`Tier.cfg .allFeatures` in `Successor/Config.lean`;
`nearspec-v3-check-successor --tier allFeatures`). Where the signed statement of record
(`D3.checkD3`/`RelD3`, `--tier signed`) or its legacy core (`D3.checkD3Core`) still excludes
something, the row's `domain_guard`/`remaining_work` says so and names the tier. Such an
exclusion is a stated signed-tier restriction, not a Gate-B gap.

**Rules applied** (tool docstring and plan §4 L0):
- `implemented` means a dispatch arm or decided path exists on `allFeatures`.
- `validated` additionally needs whole-chunk fixtures judged by cold pinned nearcore **and** a
  deliberate-breakage control.
- Promotions are derived. The tool first checks the Lean surface: an `allFeatures` flag, a
  `pBaseGlobal` arm, a `cryptoHostCall` arm with a positive and a negative fixture step, a named
  exclusion string. It then applies an evidence entry (`VALIDATION`) whose paths it checks exist.
  If the surface disappears, the row falls back to `partial`/`missing` and `--check` fails.

promotions read.

## What still stands between this inventory and Gate B

1. **UQ-014 is open.** It asks about the Wasmtime `LoadingError`/`WasmUnknownError`/resource
   `LinkError` branches at PV86 (L7, proof owed). The plan says Gate B cannot pass with an
   unresolved UQ.
2. **`L0-D3-006` is implemented but not validated.** It needs a magic-hash fixture and a breakage
   control. The plan requires every reachable row to be "implemented and validated". The branch is
   reachable on mainnet for pre-PV83 ETH-implicit accounts.
3. **The 150 `implemented` rows.** Most are signed-era rows whose text predates the fixture families:
   - 75 non-curve host arms, with text "not differentially tested in this lane";
   - 12 action tags, with text "no dedicated regression yet";
   - 31 `d2-core` guards and the D3 hook invariants.

   This pass did not re-examine them. Many are exercised by the 235,716-case corpus and the fixture
   families, but the tool records no fixture-plus-control evidence for them. Promoting them needs
   the same per-row evidence entries. Until then, Gate B's "implemented and validated" condition is
   not demonstrated for these rows.
4. **`L0-AF-fuel-certificate`** is a kept out-of-domain reason (decision in force). It fires on no
   corpus module. It stops being an exclusion only with a general `CodeCertified` proof (item 3
   residual). It is not blocking under the current decision.
5. **Residuals of promoted rows**, recorded in their `remaining_work`:
   - curve-host breakage controls are family-level, not per host;
   - O3's unconditional `Lift` needs `LayoutsWF` from admission A1;
   - the signed hand-over's buffer-to-split-parent form has no fixture;
   - genesis-main outputs are not blind-gradable, because the stored chunk extra is a claim fact.
6. W13 (all-shard mainnet replay) and the frozen-package replay (item 13) are outside L0. They are
   the replay gates of plan §5.
